Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Solutions Quasar Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of personal information entrusted to us. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information obtained through our website (solutionsquasar.ca), client communication channels, and associated digital services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when requesting a strategy call, submitting project briefs, contacting us by email or telephone, or engaging with our live chat widget. This includes your full name, business or corporate email address, phone number, company or law firm name, and project specifications.

Technical Data & Analytics: When browsing our site, our infrastructure may automatically collect technical diagnostics including IP addresses, device identifiers, browser types, and navigation timestamps to monitor platform security, protect against automated abuse, and maintain optimal performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate commercial purposes: evaluating and responding to project inquiries, architecting customized software and web solutions, managing client accounts and billing, scheduling consultations, and ensuring compliance with applicable Canadian legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Processing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. To deliver premium digital architecture, we rely on trusted, enterprise-grade third-party infrastructure adhering to strict security standards: Google Cloud / Firebase (hosting and database infrastructure), Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processing), and carrier communication infrastructure. No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement rigorous technical, organizational, and physical safeguards—including SSL/TLS 256-bit data encryption in transit, strict role-based access controls, and serverless isolation—to protect against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purpose or legal retention requirements.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Under Canadian privacy laws (PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25) and applicable international frameworks, you have the right to request access to your stored personal information, request corrections, or request complete deletion of your records. To submit a request, contact our Privacy Officer at info@solutionsquasar.ca with the subject line 'Privacy Request'.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our security standards, contact Solutions Quasar Inc., Quebec & New Brunswick, Canada. Email: info@solutionsquasar.ca | Tel: +1 506 233 7006.
